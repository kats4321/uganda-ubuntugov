# Uganda UbuntuGov

## Mission
Help Uganda’s existing government systems work together securely,
improve daily public services, and support operations from the national
level to the village.

## Core rule
Existing government institutions retain ownership of their systems
and data. UbuntuGov connects, coordinates, monitors, and supports them;
it does not replace their systems of record.

## Development status
Research and early development. Not approved for production use.

## Initial development restrictions
- Use fictional test data only.
- Do not connect to production government systems.
- Do not collect real national ID, health, land, tax, or payment data.
- Do not claim government endorsement or procurement approval.
